ABOUT US

Where creative ideas become moving stories.

The Animation & Multimedia Club brings students together to learn, experiment and create through animation, digital art, video production and interactive media.

Create. Learn. Collaborate.

A welcoming community for beginners and experienced creators.

WHAT WE ARE

Who we are and what we do

The Animation & Multimedia Club is a student-led creative community focused on building confidence, skill and imagination through visual storytelling. We bring together students who enjoy animation, digital art, video production, motion graphics and interactive media. Through hands-on learning, we help members explore ideas, experiment with tools and create work that can be shared, improved and celebrated.

MISSION

To make creative learning accessible and fun

We aim to provide a supportive space where students can develop practical skills in animation, design and multimedia while encouraging creativity, teamwork and self-expression.

VISION

To grow a strong creative community

We envision a club where students inspire each other, discover new talents and turn ideas into meaningful projects that reflect passion, curiosity and innovation.

CLUB HISTORY

Growing together since 2018

The club began in 2018 as a small group of students who wanted a space to explore animation, digital art and video editing outside class. Over time, it grew into a welcoming community for students with different skills, interests and creative goals.

2018

Club founded

Started as a student-led animation interest group.

2021

Multimedia growth

Expanded into digital art, video editing, 3D modelling and motion graphics.

2026

Creative learning hub

Developed workshops and showcases that connect more students with creative opportunities.
