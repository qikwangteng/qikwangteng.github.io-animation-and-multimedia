Policy Notice

Last updated: August 18, 2026. This policy applies to the Animation & Multimedia Club website and its related online activities.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect information you choose to provide, such as your name, email address, student details, messages, or application responses when you contact us, register for an event, join the club, or submit a form.

We may also collect limited technical information automatically, including browser type, device information, pages visited, and approximate usage data, to help keep the website secure and improve it.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to respond to enquiries, manage club membership and events, share relevant club updates, maintain the website, and comply with applicable requirements.

We only use personal information for purposes connected with the club’s activities or where you have provided consent.

3. Sharing of Information

We do not sell personal information. We may share information with club committee members, school representatives, or trusted service providers only when needed to operate the club, organise an activity, or meet legal obligations.

4. Cookies and Similar Technologies

This website may use cookies or similar tools to remember preferences, understand website performance, and improve the browsing experience. You can control cookies through your browser settings; disabling them may affect some features.

5. Data Retention and Security

We keep personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described here. We take reasonable administrative and technical steps to protect information, though no online system can be guaranteed completely secure.

6. Your Choices and Rights

You may ask to access, correct, or delete personal information we hold about you, subject to applicable requirements. You may also opt out of non-essential club communications at any time.

7. Children’s Privacy

If you are under the age required to provide consent in your location, please obtain permission from a parent, guardian, or school representative before submitting personal information through this website.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when our practices or website change. The “Last updated” date above will show when the latest version took effect.

9. Contact Us

For privacy questions or requests, please contact the Animation & Multimedia Club.
